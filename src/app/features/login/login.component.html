Email not recognized?

Make sure you're using the email address associated with your FieldWave account. Check for typos or try an alternate email.

Account locked?

After multiple failed login attempts, your account may be temporarily locked. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or contact support.

Connection Error?

If you see a connection or certificate error, your network may be blocking the connection. Try these steps:

  • Clear your browser cache and cookies
  • Try using incognito/private browsing mode
  • If on a corporate network, ask IT to whitelist droppin.shop from SSL inspection
  • Try using a different network (personal WiFi, mobile data)
