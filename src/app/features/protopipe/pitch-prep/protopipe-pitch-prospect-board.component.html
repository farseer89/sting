Pitch · Objections

Handling objections

Common pushbacks and how to reframe them using the intelligence data you have.

"We're already busy, we don't need more leads."
That's the best time to build the system — when you can afford to be selective. Right now your competitor is building a moat. When things slow down, you'll wish you'd moved when you were winning.
"We tried SEO before and it didn't work."
Most SEO fails because it targets the wrong thing — generic content, no local signals. What I'm showing you is different: review velocity, profile optimization, and a landing page that feeds Google exactly what it needs for a local pack ranking.
"We get most of our work from referrals."
Referrals are great, but the first thing a referred customer does is Google you. If they land on a 3.8★ profile with no website, you've already lost some of that warm traffic. We protect what referrals built.
"I need to think about it."
[Competitor] doesn't wait. Every month they run ads is a month they're building a stronger history with Google — quality score, click data, conversion rate. The cost to catch up increases each month you delay.
